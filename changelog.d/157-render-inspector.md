### Added

- `perf.get`'s `render_inspector` (schema 1, fields only added) reports `memory_estimate_bytes` (the game's textures, meshes and GPU buffers and the instance pools the renderer holds, with their total; not the frame's own targets, shadow maps or skinning buffers), `adapter` (name, backend, device type, driver, capability tier) and, with the new `history` parameter, the last drawn frames one by one: CPU ms, the smoothed GPU ms, the newest single-frame GPU ms with how many frames ago it was drawn, counts and upload bytes. At most 120 frames are kept, in a ring of 88-byte entries (about 10 KB) allocated once at the first drawn frame and filled in place after.
- `counts.frustum_culled_models` counts plain models outside the camera's view in the last frame, worked out only when `perf.get` asks, from the bounds the renderer keeps per model (no per-frame cost; steady while the camera is still). `counts.frustum_culled_other_models` is `null` with the reason: LOD groups, terrain, skinned models, shadow-only casters and GPU-culled static batches aren't counted. `quality_autotune` being `null` now has a reason in `unavailable` too.
- The control method `quality.set` and the MCP tool `set_graphics_quality` switch a 3D game's graphics quality preset (`low`, `medium`, `high`, `ultra`, `recommended`) while it runs, fitted to the GPU, answering with the settings before and after; it pins render scale and shadow resolution against automatic quality tuning until the game restarts, and a read-only MCP server doesn't offer it. `perf_report` takes `history`. The guide shows an agent comparing two quality levels.
