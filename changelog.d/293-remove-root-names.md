### Breaking changes
- The deprecated root names kept by the move to modules and the prelude are removed. They were re-exports, which rustc does not warn through, so check old code against this list. The crate root now holds only the modules and the prelude's items, so `use ponko::*;` brings in the prelude and the modules and no longer clashes with `d2`, `d3` or module globs (`Collider`, `RigidBody`, `QueryFilter`, `Joint`, ...). `ponko::Transform3D`, `Transform2D`, `Camera2D`, `Camera3D`, `Sprite`, `Model`, `Ui` and `UiId` stay: they are prelude names. - The crate-root spellings of the built-in systems are removed; they are in `ponko::systems`: `animate_sprites`, `apply_net_poses`, `draw_sprites`, `draw_tilemaps`, `follow_attachments`, `interpolate_physics3d`, `rebuild_spatial_grid`, `step_navmesh3d`, `step_physics`, `step_physics3d`.
- Hidden root re-exports outside the prelude are removed: `serde_json` (`reflect::json`), `Value` (`reflect::json::Value`; `json!` stays), `steamworks` (`net::steam`), `ui_layout` (`ui::layout`), `QueryFilter` and `ViewQuery` (`ecs_core::QueryFilter`, `ecs_core::ViewQuery`), `Interpolate` (`net::Interpolate`), `FieldType` (`reflect::FieldType`), `ValueSchema` (`reflect::ValueSchema`), `RawFlowView` (`flow::RawFlowView`), `RawGridView` (`spatial::RawGridView`).
- `World`'s deprecated dynamic methods are removed; call them through `world.raw()` (`insert_dynamic`, `spawn_dynamic`, `spawn_at_dynamic`, `remove_dynamic`, `insert_resource_dynamic`, `remove_resource_dynamic`, `for_each_chunk`) or `world.raw_ref()` (`get_dynamic`, `get_resource_dynamic`, `resource_layout_dynamic`, `component_by_name`, `archetypes`).
- Not changed: the C/C++ API (names and layouts) and reflected, saved and capability names (`"Transform3D"` in scene files).
