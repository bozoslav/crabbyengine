Ember Hop: content notices
==========================

Ember Hop is a reference game for the Ponko engine, built only from content
made for it. Nothing in it is taken from another work.

Everything below was made for this game (drawn and composed by short
scripts) and is Copyright (c) 2026 Leon Mamić and Ponko contributors,

Text and fonts
--------------
The game ships no font file. Its text is drawn with the default fonts bundled
into the engine by the `epaint_default_fonts` crate; their licenses are
included in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt together with every other library that is
linked into the game.
